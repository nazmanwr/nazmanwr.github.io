Six-Hole Flute Designer

Enter the scale and your tube. The calculator sizes and places the embouchure and finger holes, then draws the working drawing and 3D model.

Scale

First note is all holes closed. Each next note opens one more hole from the foot. If the last note is the octave of the first, it is played by overblowing.

Tube

Hole sizes

Holes are sized so every hole gives the same tone-hole cutoff frequency (Benade). Higher = larger holes, louder and brighter.

One per hole, in note order.

The largest hole is limited by what a fingertip can seal. About 11 mm suits most adult fingers.

Head, embouchure and air

Breath warms the bore. 25–30 °C is typical while playing.

3D model

Drag to rotate · scroll or pinch to zoom · right-drag to pan

Working drawing

The SVG is drawn in real millimetres. Print it at 100% scale and tape it along the tube as a drilling template.
